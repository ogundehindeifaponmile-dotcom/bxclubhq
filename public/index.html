About BX CLUB

BX CLUB is a luxury olfactory atelier under the umbrella of Blackxcellencee. We craft hand-poured candles, diffusers, and accessories that capture the soul of Lagos and the elegance of London. Every piece is created with intention — designed to elevate your space and spirit.

From the obsidian hours of the ancient city to the playful warmth of imperial gardens, our fragrances tell stories. We believe that scent is the most direct path to memory, to peace, and to the divine.
